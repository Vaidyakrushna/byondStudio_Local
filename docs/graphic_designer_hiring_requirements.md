# Graphic Designer — Hiring Requirements & Job Specification

**Company:** Cobblyn (Luxury Bespoke Handcrafted Footwear & Leather Goods)  
**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra (Hybrid / On-site Preferred for Physical Leather & Packaging Audits)  
**Employment Type:** Full-Time / Retainer Contract  
**Experience Level:** Mid to Senior (2–5 Years in D2C Luxury, Fashion, or Lifestyle Branding)  
**Reports To:** Founder / Head of Brand & Product  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Role Purpose
Cobblyn is an artisanal luxury footwear atelier crafting bespoke, Goodyear-welted, and made-to-order footwear for gentlemen and women. We combine classical shoemaking heritage with modern digital customization (including 3D digital customizers and doorstep master cobbler visit services).

We are seeking a **Graphic Designer & Visual Brand Custodian** who has a refined luxury aesthetic, an obsessive eye for detail, and the ability to translate premium craftsmanship into striking digital and print creatives. You will own the visual language across our web storefront, performance marketing campaigns, packaging, and brand collaterals.

---

## 2. Key Responsibilities

### **A. Digital Storefront & E-Commerce Creatives (40%)**
* **Homepage & Promotional Banners:** Design high-converting, editorial hero carousels, seasonal showcase banners, and collection teasers.
* **Mega-Menu & Category Graphics:** Create clean visual cards for navigation dropdowns, curated style tiles (Oxfords, Loafers, Double Monks, Jutis, Belts, Wallets).
* **Product Image Retouching & Post-Production:** Color-correct studio footwear shots, maintain consistent lighting/shadows across multi-angle views (side, top, front, pair), and retouch leather textures (box calf, suede, shell cordovan).
* **3D Visual Enhancements:** Work alongside our 3D customizer assets to create realistic background studio lighting mockups and composite product renders.

### **B. Performance Marketing & Social Media (30%)**
* **Ad Creatives (Meta / Google / Pinterest):** High-converting static and carousel ad sets focused on bespoke craftsmanship, custom shoe configuration, and leather care.
* **Organic Social (Instagram / Pinterest / LinkedIn):** Editorial feed posts, story templates, carousel guides (e.g. *"Anatomy of a Goodyear Welt"*, *"How to Care for Suede"*), and video title overlays.
* **Email Marketing & Newsletters:** Visually rich email templates for product launches, VIP bespoke announcements, and order follow-ups (Klaviyo / Mailchimp compatible).

### **C. Print, Packaging & Brand Collateral (20%)**
* **Signature Packaging:** Unboxing experience design, including rigid shoe boxes, debossed tissue wrap, shoe care instruction cards, and dust bag typography.
* **Physical Collateral:** Bespoke foam-box imprint measuring kit guides, artisan swatch book cards, leather authenticity certificates, and thank-you stationery.
* **Event & Showroom Material:** Lookbooks, catalog print layouts for vendor meetings, wholesale line sheets, and pop-up event banners.

### **D. Brand System Maintenance (10%)**
* Maintain and expand the Cobblyn Design Style Guide (typography hierarchy, luxury color codes, grid systems, iconography, and export standards).

## 3. Candidate Profile & Technical Qualifications

### **Required Tools & Software Proficiency**
| Category | Core Tools |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Design Suite** | Adobe Photoshop (Mastery in Retouching / Masking), Adobe Illustrator (Vector Branding & Packaging Dielines), Figma (Web Banners & Digital Assets). |
| **Motion / Video (Bonus)** | Adobe Premiere Pro / After Effects (Subtle luxury micro-animations, reels editing). |
| **Packaging & Print** | Pre-press file preparation, CMYK color profiles, Pantone spot color matching, embossing/foil stamping dielines. |

### **Qualifications & Experience**
* **2–5 years of proven experience** in graphic design, preferably with luxury fashion, premium lifestyle, leather goods, or high-growth D2C brands.
* **Exceptional Portfolio:** Must demonstrate editorial layout balance, sophisticated typography, subtle color harmonies, and high-end product photo manipulation (portfolios with clutter or generic templates will not be shortlisted).
* Strong understanding of typography pairing (e.g., editorial serifs with minimalist grotesque sans-serifs).
* Familiarity with responsive web aspect ratios (desktop vs. mobile viewports).

## 4. Brand Aesthetic & Visual Guidelines (Cobblyn DNA)

```
[ Cobblyn Visual Pillars ]
  ├── 1. Timeless Luxury    : Serene, understated, editorial (No screaming discount banners)
  ├── 2. Tactile Detail     : High-resolution leather grain, stitch clarity, patina depth
  ├── 3. Structured Polish   : Clean white-space, symmetrical grids, golden ratio balance
  └── 4. Palette Harmony    : Deep Obsidian, Warm Ecru / Oatmeal, Burnt Sienna, Antique Brass
```

* **Tone:** Sophisticated, Artisanal, Bespoke, Heritage Modern.
* **What to Avoid:** Heavy gradients, cartoonish vectors, generic discount badges, oversaturated colors, cluttered compositions.

## 6. Hiring & Screening Process

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Step 1: Portfolio Review] --> B[Step 2: 20-min Intro Call]
    B --> C[Step 3: Paid Design Challenge]
    C --> D[Step 4: Design Review & Culture Fit]
    D --> E[Step 5: Final Offer]
```

1. **Step 1: Portfolio Screening:** Review past work for fashion/luxury alignment.
2. **Step 2: Introductory Screening (20 Mins):** Discuss experience, creative process, and software workflow.
3. **Step 3: Paid Practical Assignment (24–48 Hours):** A realistic 2-part test to evaluate real-world craft and speed.
4. **Step 4: Technical & Culture Fit Interview (45 Mins):** Review the submitted assignment source files, layers, and design decisions with the Founder.
5. **Step 5: Offer Rollout.**
